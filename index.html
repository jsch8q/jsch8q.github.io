Jiseok Chae

I am a postdoctoral researcher, formally appointed at the Natural Science Research Institute at KAIST, but affiliated with and mainly working with the PDE & AI Lab at Yonsei University, led by Prof. Dohyun Kwon. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from KAIST under the supervision of Prof. Donghwan Kim. Before that, I completed my bachelor’s degree in Mathematical Sciences from KAIST, with a minor in Computer Science.

I study optimization theory and algorithms for machine learning. Recently, I also got interested in how optimization methods work in real world machine learning applications (implicit biases, generalization properties, etc.). If you are interested in collaborating with me, or if you want to know more about anything that is on this website, feel free to contact me by email.
